What we treat

Whatever’s happened, we can help

If it hurts, it’s broken or it’s swollen, get in touch. These are the emergencies we see most.

Severe toothache

Throbbing or constant pain that painkillers aren’t touching.

Broken or chipped tooth

From a fall, a knock or biting something hard.

Abscess or swelling

A swollen jaw, gum boil or signs of infection.

Lost filling or crown

A gap, sharp edge or sensitivity where a filling or crown used to be.

Knocked-out tooth

Keep it moist in milk and call us straight away. Time matters.

Bleeding gums

Bleeding that won’t settle, or after an injury or extraction.

Questions

Good to know

How quickly can I be seen?

We offer same-day appointments. Call us or book online and we’ll ring you back, usually within 30 minutes, to confirm a time today.

How much does it cost?

A fixed fee of £200 covers your emergency consultation and treatment. There are no hidden costs.

Do I need to be registered with you?

No. We’re a private emergency service, so anyone can book, whether or not you have a regular dentist.

When are you open?

Every day, including weekends, from 9am to 10pm.

Is there parking?

Yes, there’s free on-site parking at 80 New North Road, just minutes from Huddersfield town centre.

When should I go to A&E instead?

If you have swelling that’s affecting your breathing, swallowing or vision, heavy bleeding that won’t stop, or a serious facial injury, go to A&E or call 999. For urgent NHS advice, call 111.
